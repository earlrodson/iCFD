The Moment iCFD Started

It Started With 12 Questions

My wife gave me a list of 12 topics she wanted to understand, and asked me to write explanations so she could study them.

Because Cebuano was difficult for her, I initially created the material in Tagalog and English.

"Why stop at 12 articles when I can build something that can become a library?"

That became the beginning of iCFD.

Theological Etymology

Sometimes the Meaning Is in the Word

ekklesia

Greek → assembly, called-out ones → "Church"

latria

Worship due to God alone

dulia

Veneration given to saints

Theotokos

"God-bearer" — title of Mary

kecharitomene

"Full of grace" — Luke 1:28

The objective is to help the defender understand terminology behind theological arguments — not merely memorize conclusions.

18
Why This Architecture

Designed to Start Small and Scale With Evidence

  • Cloud-hosted, managed infrastructure — no servers to maintain ourselves
  • Progressive Web App — one codebase, works on any device, installable
  • Centralized database with authentication and file storage
  • Offline-capable client
  • Infrastructure that scales up only when usage requires it
Do not pay for capacity we do not yet need.

What iCFD Is Not

iCFD Does Not Replace the Church

It does not replace:

Priests

Catechists

CFD leaders

Personal formation

Parish communities

The Magisterium

Technology should serve formation — not replace it.

iCFD is a tool for formation and access to Catholic resources. It does not replace the teaching authority of the Church or formation provided by priests, catechists, and CFD leaders.

A Tool to Support Our Educators

iCFD gives our educators a focused way to bring good, guided content to CFD members — a new way of teaching, not a replacement for it.
